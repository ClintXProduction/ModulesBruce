Before You Connect

Quick checks to avoid damaged hardware and confusing pin conflicts.

01 / Confirm voltage

Check VCC range and logic level. A module accepting 5 V power may still output 5 V logic.

02 / Check GPIO use

CYD boards may reserve pins for the display, touch, SD card, audio, or onboard peripherals.

03 / Share ground

Most modules need a common GND with the ESP32. Power motors and high-current LEDs separately when required.
